Tap JUMP to start or select. Hold directions to move.
Down + Jump in mid-air: ground-pound. Auto-run frees a thumb.

Keyboard controls & tips
ARROWS / WASD Move (Go Back Anytime!)  |  Z / SPACE Jump  |  X / SHIFT / C Run & Shoot Fireball
DOWN + JUMP (Mid-air) Ground-Pound  |  DOWN (On Ice) Belly-Slide  |  H Hat  |  N Next Night  |  M Mute  |  P Pause
On touch screens, JUMP selects menu items and RUN / FIRE goes back.

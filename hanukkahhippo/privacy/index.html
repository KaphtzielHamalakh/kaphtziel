Privacy policy

Effective November 22, 2026

What the app collects

Hanukkah Hippo does not collect, transmit, or share personal data. The game does not require an account or an internet connection, and it has no ads, tracking, analytics, or in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

The app stores game progress and preferences, such as sound and haptic settings, locally on your device. Kaphtziel Studios does not receive this information. You can remove the app’s local data by deleting the app from your device.

External links and support email

Opening the privacy link takes you to this website outside the game. The website host may process standard request information to serve and secure the site. If you email support, we use the address and information you send to respond. Please do not include passwords or other sensitive information. You can contact us to ask about or request deletion of your support correspondence.

Support

For help or to report an issue, email rabbibennewman@gmail.com. If you’re reporting a bug, tell us your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

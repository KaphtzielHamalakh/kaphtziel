Space Pig 2.0 Privacy and Support

This policy covers version 2.0 of Space Pig for iPhone and iPad.

Playing the game

Space Pig does not require a game account, display ads, or use advertising trackers. The game saves your progress and preferences on your device and does not send them to the developer.

Purchases and restoration

Apple processes the optional Galactic Rescue Expansion purchase. The game uses Apple’s StoreKit to verify access to the expansion and to restore access to an eligible purchase. The developer does not receive your payment details through the game.

Contact

For help with Space Pig or questions about this policy, use the developer support form. If you write to us, we use the information you submit to respond to your request.

Asael Genesis passage candidates and acoustic research (2026-11-25)

Primary catalog: Hebrew Wikisource contributors, Miqra according to the Masorah /
Vayavinu Bamikra / Parashat Bereshit, page2460, revision2898373:
https://he.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=2898373
Catalog material: CC BY-SA4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Ten unchanged original Ogg Vorbis recordings are explicitly cataloged as Asael
(עשהאל), with source melody and pronunciation labels kept separately.
Commons file pages individually identify CC BY-SA3.0 (GFDL migration=relicense):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
Original URLs, page revisions, byte counts, SHA1, SHA256 and decoded clocks are
retained in docs/ASAEL-GENESIS-PASSAGES.json and the pinned source pack.
No original audio is included in this new pack. The app loads unchanged sources
on request and verifies their checksums before playback. No endorsement implied.

Acoustic model: imvladikon/wav2vec2-large-xlsr-53-hebrew, revision
3dd308dda70b20507a6920b459052635973ae3cd, Apache-2.0.
https://huggingface.co/imvladikon/wav2vec2-large-xlsr-53-hebrew
The original model card, model-input hashes and research code are in the pack.
Weights are not redistributed. Decoding/resampling is analysis only; original
audio is unchanged, and no sound output or speech synthesis is generated.

New project code and proposals: CC0. Hebrew reader text remains the exact
Tanach.us UXLC text with its existing provenance and license; no recognizer
spelling replaces the text, accents, joiners, stress or word identifiers.

Two source-stated passage candidates remain unreviewed. Three other catalog
ranges conflict with approximate recognizer evidence and stay withheld; no
corrected passage is assigned. Model support is not approved word/syllable
timing, phonemes, musical notes, a complete tradition or a singing voice.
Independent source-content, edition, pronunciation and cantorial review remain
pending. The original full feature inventory is retained.

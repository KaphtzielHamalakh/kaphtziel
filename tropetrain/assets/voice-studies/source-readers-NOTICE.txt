Consult the selected original file page for full authorship and any additional offered license. The app chooses one documented reusable license per original; it does not infer rights from the reader catalog.

Featured five Chukat sources: catalog reader Nombody; the original file pages attribute the recordings to Joshua Hosseinof and declare own work / PD-self. Literal melody labels are ספרדי-ירושלמי, ספרדי-מרוקאית, ספרדי-טורקית, אשכנזי and ספרדי-פורטוגזית. Four pronunciation labels are ספרדי. The Ashkenazic file has contradictory source claims: MAM says מעורב (mixed), while Commons says Ashkenazi pronunciation. Both are retained. Creator attribution, catalog reader identity and independently verified performance identity are separate claims. These are not Kinnor presets or reviewed complete traditions.

PD-self reference: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Template:PD-self . Source dedications are in each pinned file page. No copyright license URL was supplied by the metadata for these six originals; the app links the documented dedication template and original page rather than inventing a Creative Commons license.

Orel Zion Genesis opening: catalog reader אוראל, original attribution orel zion, PD-self. Its numeric catalog passage note is retained but content/edition verification remains pending; no word coverage is inferred from that note.

Audio modifications: NONE. Audio is not bundled by this increment. On request the selected unchanged original is downloaded, checked against the pinned byte count and source SHA1 (and SHA256 where locally verified), then decoded at its own original duration. Six originals (five Nombody plus Orel) were privately downloaded and decoded with soundfile 0.13.1 / libsndfile 1.2.2 without audio output. All exact durations matched Commons metadata. This checks bytes and clocks, not pronunciation, melody, verse coverage or cantorial interpretation.

No missing melody/pronunciation combination is synthesized. Different recordings are not swapped to fill a missing pair. Available-recording buttons explicitly choose both documented labels. Recording-specific seconds spans and existing personal word drafts retain canonical original IDs. Full contextual melody grammars, accepted word/syllable/phoneme alignment, corresponding reusable vocal material and independent cantorial/phonological review remain required for whole-Torah generated chanting.

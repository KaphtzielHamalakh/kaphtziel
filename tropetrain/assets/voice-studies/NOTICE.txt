Asael opening-passage word-support research, November22,2026.
Two original chanted performances remain unchanged under their existing CC-BY-SA3.0 attribution above. Optional word-support estimates are original project analysis; original marked UXLC words and stable IDs are retained. They are unreviewed model-token support, not confirmed word/syllable/phoneme/note timings or a new singing voice. Source/model/recipe pins and limits are in docs/ASAEL-WORD-SUPPORT.json and assets/voice-studies/asael-word-support-sources.zip.
Analysis uses imvladikon/wav2vec2-large-xlsr-53-hebrew, revision3dd308dda70b20507a6920b459052635973ae3cd, with an explicit Apache2.0 model-card declaration (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). The checkpoint and its audio training material are not bundled. It is a modern speech recognizer used to analyze original chant; no model speech replaces singing. Mean posterior/direct-letter agreement is uncalibrated, and many words lack direct support. No source edition, historical pronunciation/melody grammar or qualified acoustic review is certified.

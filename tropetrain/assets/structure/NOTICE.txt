The source uses the public-domain Westminster Leningrad Codex, version 4.20.
The n attributes record its edited cantillation divisions. The source README
and OshbVerse documentation describe their nested dot-separated paths.

TropeTrain derives phrase ranges from those paths only when every preferred
reading word, accent, vowel and punctuation in a whole verse agrees with the
unchanged UXLC reading. Morphology separators and separately encoded source
maqaf, paseq and sof pasuq are attached to their source word for comparison.
No source or displayed Hebrew string is rewritten. Other editions and changed
marks are withheld. Complete source XML, its license and README are preserved
in oshb-torah-source.zip for reproducible builds.

The compact derived packet and comparison are adaptations by this project,
under CC BY 4.0. New selection and validation code is original. No endorsement
by the Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible Project is implied.

These are source phrase boundaries, with independent cantorial review pending.
They do not provide melody pitches, sung syllable alignment, a pronunciation
voice or equivalence to a historical Kinnor preset. Selected recordings retain
their own melody and pronunciation; instrumental studies keep their own notes.

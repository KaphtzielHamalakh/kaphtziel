The MP3 and timing file are copied byte-for-byte, without editing, pitch shifting or time stretching. The source text is WLC 4.14 (2010), as distributed in the 2012 PocketTorah release, whose header states freely available. Morphology slashes are removed only from display Hebrew, XML editorial codes are stored separately, and original source tokens and marks remain in the alignment data/source excerpt. UXLC strings remain separate and unchanged.

The 469 word positions align: 458 exact display strings; ten joiner-only differences; one accent difference at Genesis 1:11 word 5 (WLC zakef katon, UXLC revia). Word boundaries are source-provided and have not been independently reviewed acoustically. No phonemes, syllables or pitches were inferred. No musical or pronunciation review or reusable synthesis profile is claimed.

Whole-Torah reference library (v0.7)

The catalog maps all 79,985 UXLC reader units to 79,986 original WLC source words in 378 Torah recordings (54 portions, seven aliyot each). Source file checksums, original paths, per-recording Archive filename/byte count/SHA-1, review flags and both exact Hebrew editions are retained in data/references/torah-library.json.gz. The compact format removes only repeated derived strings; reference-library.js expands original token columns without Unicode normalization.

Original biblical XML, original aliquot/division metadata and 378 unmodified timing files are included in data/references/torah-sources.zip. SOURCE-MANIFEST.json in that archive records all 384 original files, their Git blob and SHA-256 checksums and original 2012 commit. Biblical XML headers state freely available. Original timing/division creative content retains the creator's CC BY-SA 2.5 notice; the derived source comparison and reference playback adaptation are CC BY-SA 3.0. No application code from PocketTorah is included.

The other MP3s load only after an explicit user choice. They are downloaded from the original Archive collection without editing and checked against the collection's original byte counts and SHA-1 before use. Verified copies can be kept on the device. The opening recording remains bundled and unmodified. No pitch shifting, time stretching, synthesized pronunciation, musical transcription or individual-reader identification is claimed.

370 source timing files have matching counts and strictly increasing starts; this is structural correspondence, not acoustic or musical review. Eight files have original timing errors: Shemot 1/5/7, Tetzaveh 1/3, Shmini 6, Nasso 4 and Eikev 3. Their full original takes are available without timed word highlighting; missing/invalid word boundaries are not guessed. The catalog retains those errors for review.

WLC/UXLC chapter letter sequences correspond, with source-mark differences, 393 word references affected by verse renumbering and a two-WLC-word / one-UXLC-unit boundary at Numbers 12:9. All original tokens, marks, editorial codes and written variants remain distinct. Source records and playback adaptation do not establish an equivalent Kinnor preset, independently reviewed tune or pronunciation profile.

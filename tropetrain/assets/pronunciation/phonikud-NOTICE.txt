TropeTrain implements an original adaptation of the modern inventory to the existing Phonetic MAM source-category plans. It does not execute the upstream whole-word phonemizer, use its invented annotations, normalize biblical marks or select spoken-sheva/suffix decisions. Exact Hebrew, source syllables/stress/branches/shared consonants and musical controls are retained. Correspondence adaptation, phonetic transitions and linguistic/cantorial review remain pending. Accepted phonemes, engine phones, acoustic timing, singing voice and Kinnor equivalence remain unassigned. The source authors do not endorse this adaptation.

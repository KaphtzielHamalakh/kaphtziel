The five source JSON files are unchanged in the source ZIP. New work: source rows linked to unchanged UXLC word IDs by complete-verse letter correspondence, documented implicit reading forms and ordered anchors. Remaining regions require two checked complete-verse anchors; only exact groups present on every best monotonic alignment are added. No spelling substitutions or arbitrary tie breaks are used. Original Hebrew variants, source columns, NFD transcription strings, stress, superscript-e and alternate-reading objects are retained. Ambiguous/unmatched words have no assigned transcription. The reader's source Hebrew, accents, default draft syllables, printed-score notes and original recording timings remain unchanged.

The source names its text transcriptions Sephardic and Ashkenazic. This does not identify them with six historical Kinnor Accent labels, change the pronunciation of a recording, supply phonemes/audio timing or implement singing synthesis. Independent linguistic and mapping review remains pending. No endorsement is implied.
